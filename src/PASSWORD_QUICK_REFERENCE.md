# Password Authentication - Quick Reference Guide

## 🔐 For New Users

### Creating Your Account
1. **Choose a Username** (3-20 characters, letters, numbers, underscores)
2. **Create a Strong Password:**
   - Minimum 8 characters
   - Include uppercase letters (A-Z)
   - Include lowercase letters (a-z)
   - Include special characters (!@#$%^&*()_+-=[]{}; ':"\\|,.<>/?)
3. **Confirm Your Password**
4. **Save Your UUID!** - This is displayed after account creation

### Logging In
- **Username:** Your chosen username
- **Password:** The password you created
- No need to remember your UUID for daily login!

### If You Forget Your Password
1. Click "Forgot Password? Use UUID to Reset"
2. Enter your UUID (format: ABC-123-XY-7890)
3. Create a new password
4. You'll be automatically logged in

## 📋 Password Requirements

### Minimum Requirements
- ✅ At least 8 characters long
- ✅ At least one UPPERCASE letter
- ✅ At least one lowercase letter
- ✅ At least one special character (!@#$%^&*()_+-=[]{}; ':"\\|,.<>/?)

### Password Strength Levels
- 🔴 **Weak:** Minimum requirements not met
- 🟡 **Medium:** Minimum requirements met
- 🔵 **Strong:** Good password complexity
- 🟢 **Very Strong:** Excellent security

### Tips for Strong Passwords
1. **Use a mix of characters:** Combine letters, numbers, and symbols
2. **Make it longer:** 12+ characters is better
3. **Avoid common words:** Don't use dictionary words or names
4. **Make it unique:** Don't reuse passwords from other sites
5. **Consider a passphrase:** "Coffee@Morning2024!" is strong and memorable

## 🆘 Important Information

### Your UUID (User Recovery Code)
- **What is it?** A unique code in format: ABC-123-XY-7890
- **Where to find it?** Your Profile page (when logged in)
- **Why save it?** It's your ONLY way to reset a forgotten password
- **Keep it safe!** Write it down or store it securely

### UUID Locations
1. **After Account Creation:** Displayed on success screen
2. **In Your Profile:** Visible in the "Password Recovery UUID" card
3. **Tip:** Take a screenshot or write it down immediately!

## 🔍 Troubleshooting

### "Password does not meet requirements"
- Check that your password has:
  - At least 8 characters
  - Uppercase AND lowercase letters
  - At least one special character
- Watch the password strength meter for real-time feedback

### "Passwords do not match"
- Retype both password fields carefully
- Make sure Caps Lock is off
- Use the "Show Password" button to verify

### "Invalid UUID format"
- UUID must be in format: ABC-123-XY-7890
- Check for typos
- Make sure you're using the correct UUID from your profile

### "Username not found"
- Verify you're entering the correct username
- Usernames are case-sensitive
- Contact support if issues persist

### "Incorrect password"
- Double-check your password
- Try using the "Show Password" button
- If forgotten, use UUID to reset password
