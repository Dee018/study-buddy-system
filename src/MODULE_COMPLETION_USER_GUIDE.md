# Module Completion User Guide

## How Module Completion Works

### What Makes a Module "Complete"?

A module is considered **100% complete** when you have finished:

✅ **All Lessons** - Read and understood all lesson content  
✅ **All Exercises** - Submitted correct code for every exercise  
✅ **Module Project** - Submitted your final project code

### Visual Indicators of Completion

#### ✅ Completed Modules Show:
- **Green Checkmark Icon** (✓) instead of book icon
- **"Completed" Badge** in purple/primary color
- **100% Progress Bar** with gradient effect
- **"Review" Text** instead of "Explore"
- **Subtle background tint** indicating completion

#### 🔄 In-Progress Modules Show:
- **Book Icon** or **Flame Icon** (if current)
- **Progress Percentage** (0% to 99%)
- **"Current" Badge** (if currently working on it)
- **"Explore" Text** for navigation

#### 🔒 Locked Modules Show:
- **Lock Icon** (🔒)
- **Muted Colors** (grayed out)
- **"Locked" Text**
- **Tooltip** explaining what needs to be completed first

## Your Progress is Safe! 🛡️

### Progress Never Resets
- ✅ Completed modules **stay completed forever**
- ✅ Progress **never returns to zero**
- ✅ All your work is **automatically saved**
- ✅ Safe across **browser sessions**
- ✅ Works **offline and online**

### Automatic Saving
Your progress is saved:
- ⏱️ **Every 10 seconds** automatically
- ✅ **On every lesson completion**
- ✅ **On every exercise submission**
- ✅ **On every project submission**
- 🌐 **When you navigate pages**
- 🚪 **When you close your browser**
- 📱 **When you switch tabs**

You **never** need to manually save!

## Reviewing Completed Content

### How to Review Completed Lessons

1. Click on a **completed module** (with ✓ checkmark)
2. Navigate to the **Lessons tab**
3. Click on any lesson to read it again
4. The lesson will show a **"Completed"** badge
5. Click **"Back to Module"** when done

**What You Can Do:**
- ✅ Re-read all lesson content
- ✅ Review concepts and examples
- ✅ Refresh your understanding
- ✅ Access the content anytime

**What You Cannot Do:**
- ❌ Cannot "uncomplete" a lesson
- ❌ No quiz retaking (first attempt counts)

### How to Review Completed Exercises

1. Click on a **completed module**
2. Navigate to the **Practice tab**
3. Click on a completed exercise
4. You'll see a **green "Exercise Completed ✓" banner**

**What You See:**
- ✅ Your **original submitted code** loaded
- ✅ The **exercise requirements**
- ✅ The **expected output**
- ✅ **Read-only code editor** (disabled)
- ✅ **"Return to Hub"** button

**Important Notes:**
- 🔒 Code editor is **read-only** (you cannot edit)
- ✓ Shows your **exact submitted solution**
- 🚫 **No re-submission** possible
- ⭐ **XP already awarded** (one-time only)

### How to Review Completed Projects

1. Click on a **completed module**
2. Navigate to the **Project tab**
3. View your completed project

**What You Can Access:**
- ✅ Your **submitted project code**
- ✅ All **project requirements**
- ✅ **Grading criteria**
- ✅ Original **starter code reference**

**Features:**
- 📖 Full project specification
- 💻 Your submitted solution visible
- 🎯 Points earned displayed
- ⬅️ "Back" button to return

## Frequently Asked Questions

### ❓ Can I reset a completed module?
**No.** Once completed, modules remain complete to preserve your achievement and learning record.

### ❓ Can I re-submit exercises or projects?
**No.** Submissions are final. The read-only code shows your original work for review purposes.

### ❓ What if I want to try different solutions?
You can practice similar problems in the playground or create new coding practice outside the module system.

### ❓ Will my progress sync across devices?
If using **Supabase integration**: Yes!  
If using **localStorage only**: Progress is device-specific.

### ❓ What happens if I clear my browser data?
**With Supabase**: Progress is safe in the cloud  
**Without Supabase**: Progress may be lost (export backups regularly!)

### ❓ Can I export my completed code?
Yes! Use the **Export Progress** feature in your profile to backup all your data including submitted code.

### ❓ Will completed modules expire?
**Never!** Your completed modules remain accessible permanently.

### ❓ Can I see code from all my exercises together?
This feature is planned for future updates! You'll be able to view a portfolio of all your submitted code.
